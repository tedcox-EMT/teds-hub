1200-12-01-.09

Ground Invalid Vehicle Standards

1200-12-01 · General Rules

Invalid (non-emergency, non-monitored) transport vehicles are a different class than ambulances.

  • Invalid services are licensed and permitted under their own category.
  • An invalid vehicle is not a substitute 911 ambulance.
