Second county license / posting

A State-issued service license is required for each county where the service has a base of operations. Permits ride on that license. Posting into another county's protected area needs that government's authorization.

  1. No ambulance or cot-equipped transport business operates in Tennessee without a service license. Cite 1200-12-01-.14.
  2. A license is issued only to the named applicant and only for the listed base and substations. It is not transferable and expires June 30.
  3. Each base of operations needs a State-issued service license for the county where it sits. The official EMS site says the same: a separate service license is required for each county in which the service has a base of operations, and ambulances are permitted to the license they are assigned.
  4. A posted spare that is not a base of operations is not automatically a second county license. Ask whether the site is a listed base or substation on the license. If it is, that county needs its own license. Cite 1200-12-01-.14.
  5. The service may not post into another county's protected service area against local ordinance without that government's authorization. Cite 1200-12-01-.14.
  6. Patients are not transported until the license is issued. Using a second party to dodge a denied license or an inspection is expressly forbidden in .14.
  7. Initial service applications use PH-3987. Confirm the current packet on the state EMS site before you file.
