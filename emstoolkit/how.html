How to use

A working copy of Tennessee EMS rules, forms, and Office contacts for service directors.

What this is

  • Search answers a question against Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 1200-12 and the Office roster.
  • Rules is the full section list. Tap a title to open the digest.
  • Forms is the state applications and notices.
  • Office is the Nashville numbers, regional consultants, and the published Board roster.

This is not a state website. Confirm names, fees, and forms on tn.gov/health/licensure/ems before you send a letter.

How to look something up

  1. Open Search. Type the question the way you would ask it in the office.
  2. Press Search. Read the matching sections, forms, and lookups.
  3. Tap a section cite to open the digest. Tap a form number to open the state file.
  4. Home has ready lookups for the questions directors ask most.

Put it on your phone

Open this site in the phone browser and add it to the home screen. It opens like an app.

iPhone

  1. Open the site in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (square with the arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open the site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots in the corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, then confirm.
