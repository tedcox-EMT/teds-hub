When are the Board meetings

1200-12 does not print the calendar. Dates, agendas, minutes, and the livestream are on the state EMS site. Chapter 06 is what the Board does when it sits.

  1. Open tn.gov/health/licensure/ems and use Meeting Schedule and Meeting Minutes. That is the controlling calendar. It is not in the compilation PDF.
  2. 1200-12-06-.01 is the chair's role. 1200-12-06-.02 is contested-case procedure before the Board. 1200-12-06-.03 is petitions for reconsideration and stays.
  3. The Board adopts rules and hears contested cases. A regional consultant inspection or an Office letter is not a Board meeting and is not a Board order.
  4. If you need a rule change, a contested case, or an agenda item, put the request in writing to the Office and keep it in the service file.
  5. Do not treat a hallway answer as a substitute for a Board action under chapter 06.
