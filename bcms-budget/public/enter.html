How this works
Start from last month so the chart of accounts copies forward. Then replace year-to-date spent (and official department totals) from the new Statement of Expenditures and Encumbrances. Saving the same fiscal year, month, and report date updates that snapshot. A new report date inserts a new one. County finance is official.
